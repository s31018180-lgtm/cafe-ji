Our Heritage & Passion

Crafted with Heart,
Brewed to Perfection.

Aura Café was born from a simple belief: that a truly memorable cup of coffee is an intimate ritual that restores the spirit.

Founded in 2018, our journey started in the misty high-altitude slopes of Coorg and Chikmagalur, where we partnered directly with sustainable, shade-grown coffee estates. Every cherry is handpicked at peak ripeness, roasted in-house in small batches, and crafted by passionate baristas.

Whether you are catching up with cherished friends, diving into your favourite book in a sunlit corner, or sealing a creative deal, our warm cream aesthetic and soothing acoustics make you feel completely at ease.
